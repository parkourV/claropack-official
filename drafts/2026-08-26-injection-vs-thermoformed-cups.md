## 正文大纲与初稿

### 1. Two Processes, Two Very Different Cups
啤 Injection molding 与 thermoforming 是饮品杯最主流的两种成型工艺。注塑（injection molding）将熔融 PP 注入模具，形成厚壁、硬挺的杯体；热成型（thermoforming）把 PET 片材加热后吸塑成型，得到轻薄、透亮的杯体。工艺决定了成品的重量、刚性和适用饮品。

### 2. Injection-Molded PP Cups: Thick, Rigid, Heat-Capable
注塑 PP 杯壁厚、手感接近可重复使用的硬杯，能承受热饮、运输不易变形。真实规格显示同为 95mm 口径，注塑 PP 杯的克重明显高于热成型 PET 杯（见规格表）。适合高端奶茶、热饮及需要“质感”的品牌。

### 3. Thermoformed PET Cups: Light, Clear, Cold-Only
热成型 PET 杯以片材吸塑成型，杯壁更薄、克重更低、透明度极高，非常适合冷饮的视觉呈现，但不耐热。真实规格显示 PET 系列克重区间明显低于注塑 PP。

### 4. How the Process Affects Your Sourcing Decision
- 需要热饮 / 厚实手感 → 注塑 PP
- 需要极致透亮 / 轻量冷饮 → 热成型 PET
- 需要独特杯型 → 注塑可开专属模具（模具周期见 posts.jsx 已述 25-35 天，其余细节待工厂确认）

### 5. Same Caliber, Different Build
强调：工艺不同不影响口径匹配。只要口径一致（如 95mm），封盖与封口膜仍可通用。引导内链到「口径标准化」和「封口膜选型」两篇。

## 建议 FAQ（3 条）

1. **Q: Why is an injection PP cup heavier than a PET cup of similar size?**
   A: Because injection molding produces a thicker, rigid wall. For example, the listed 95mm PP cups run 14-22 g, while the listed 95mm PET cups are 14-16 g for larger capacities. The process, not just the size, drives the weight.

2. **Q: Can I use the same lids for injection and thermoformed cups?**
   A: Yes, as long as the rim caliber matches. Lid fit depends on rim diameter (e.g. 95mm), not on how the cup was manufactured. Confirm the exact cup model before ordering lids.

3. **Q: Which process is better for hot drinks?**
   A: Injection-molded PP cups handle hot drinks (PP tolerates up to about 100°C), while thermoformed PET cups are cold-only. Choose the process by your drink temperature and desired feel.
