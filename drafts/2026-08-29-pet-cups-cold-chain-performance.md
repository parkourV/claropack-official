## 正文大纲与初稿

### 1. Why PET Suits Cold Beverages
PET(聚对苯二甲酸乙二醇酯)以其玻璃般的透明度和适宜冷饮的材料特性,成为冰饮、气泡水、鲜果茶的首选。已核实材料说明:PET 适用于冷饮及常温饮品(耐温约 60°C),不适合热饮——这正好对应冷链与冰饮的使用温区。

### 2. Clarity Under Condensation
冷饮杯在低温下外壁易产生冷凝水。PET 的高透明度使得即便有水珠,层次分明的饮品(如分层奶茶、水果茶)依然醒目。适合货架陈列与外卖视觉呈现。

### 3. Gram Weight and Stacking in Transit
冷链运输中杯体需堆叠且承受搬运。真实克重(见规格表)从 74mm 3oz 的 3.8g 到 107mm 32oz 的 20g 递增,较大杯型壁体更厚,有助于堆叠时保持形状。**具体抗跌落/堆叠承重需工厂确认**。

### 4. Caliber and Carton Planning for Cold-Chain
冷链仓配讲究装箱密度与周转。真实装箱量:小杯(74mm)每箱 2000pcs,主力 90-98mm 每箱 1000pcs,大杯(107mm)每箱 600pcs。据此规划冷库暂存与配送批量。

### 5. Match Lids and Film for Leak-Free Cold Delivery
冷饮外送需防漏。PET 杯(90-98mm)可配 PET 平盖/圆顶盖或使用 PET 封口膜封口。引导内链到「封口膜选型」与「杯盖口径」两篇。

## 建议 FAQ（3 条）

1. **Q: Can PET cups handle cold and frozen drinks?**
   A: PET is designed for cold and ambient beverages (up to about 60°C), which covers iced drinks and chilled service. For its low-temperature limit in frozen conditions, please confirm with us.

2. **Q: Do PET cups stay clear when covered in condensation?**
   A: Yes. PET has glass-like clarity, so layered drinks and fruit teas stay visually appealing even when the cup surface has condensation from cold service.

3. **Q: How should I plan cartons for cold-chain distribution?**
   A: Carton quantity varies by size — 2,000 pcs for 74mm, 1,000 pcs for 90-98mm, and 600 pcs for 107mm. Use these to plan cold-storage batching and delivery loads.
