## 正文大纲与初稿

### 1. Why Delivery Leaks Cost More Than a Refund
Food-delivery platforms have made drinks a major revenue channel for boba and coffee shops, but a drink that arrives spilled means an instant refund, a bad review, and often a lost repeat customer. Leak protection is not one product; it is three layers working together: the **lid**, the **seal**, and the **carrier**. Getting all three right is what makes takeaway drinks travel reliably.

### 2. Layer 1 — Choose the Right Lid for the Drink
The lid is the first line of defense, and the right lid depends on the drink:

- **Flat lids** (PET, ~2.2 g, fits 90-98mm) are the cost-effective default for standard iced coffee and tea.
- **Dome / half-round lids** (PET, ~3.5 g, fits 90-98mm) leave headroom for whipped cream, fruit, or tall toppings so the topping does not smear the lid.
- **Sipper / strawless lids** (PET, ~3.2 g, 90mm) remove the straw hole risk and suit plastic-reduction markets.
- **Panda / specialty lids** (PET, ~4.5 g, 90mm) add branded visual appeal for signature drinks.

Our PET lids feature a "Snap-Lock" rim design to help prevent accidental pops and leaks during delivery (as noted in our lid compatibility guide). Whichever lid you choose, the rule is the same: the lid diameter must match the cup caliber exactly — a 3mm mismatch is enough to cause leaks.

### 3. Layer 2 — Seal It: When to Use Sealing Film Instead of a Lid
For drinks that are shaken, iced, or full to the brim, a heat-sealed film is often more spill-resistant than a snap-on lid, because it bonds a flat barrier across the entire rim. Match the film to the cup material — PET film for PET cups, PP film for PP cups — across the 90-98mm range. For a full breakdown, see our sealing film and machine guide. Film also doubles as branded real estate when custom printed.

> 决策提示：饮品满杯/含冰/需摇匀 → 优先封口膜；含高顶料（奶盖、鲜果） → 优先 dome 盖。两者也可组合（先封膜再扣 dome 盖）。

### 4. Layer 3 — Secure It: Cup Carriers and Trays
Even a perfectly sealed cup will tip over in a delivery bag without a carrier. Common formats include multi-cup trays (2-cup / 4-cup), single-cup bag holders, and cup sleeves for grip and insulation. We list straws, carriers and sleeves as available alongside our cup lines.

### 5. A Practical Leak-Proof Checklist
Before you finalize your delivery packaging:
1. Confirm the lid caliber matches the cup rim exactly (90 / 92 / 95 / 98mm).
2. For high-fill or iced drinks, add sealing film matched to the cup material.
3. For topping-heavy drinks, use a dome lid for clearance.
4. Add a carrier or tray so cups stay upright in the bag.
5. Order samples and run a real delivery-bag shake test before bulk ordering.

## 3 条 FAQ（初稿）

1. **Q: Is sealing film or a snap-on lid better for delivery?**
   A: For drinks filled to the brim, iced, or shaken, a heat-sealed film usually resists spills better because it bonds a flat barrier across the whole rim. For drinks with tall toppings like whipped cream or fresh fruit, a dome lid gives clearance. Many shops combine both — seal the film, then add a dome lid. Match the film material to the cup: PET film for PET cups, PP film for PP cups.

2. **Q: Why does my lid keep popping off during delivery?**
   A: The most common cause is a caliber mismatch — the lid diameter must equal the cup rim exactly (90, 92, 95 or 98mm). Even a 3mm gap causes loose fit and leaks. Our PET lids use a Snap-Lock rim to reduce accidental pops; confirm your lid and cup calibers match before ordering.

3. **Q: Do you supply cup carriers and trays for delivery?**
   A: Yes, we list straws, carriers and sleeves alongside our cup lines. Exact carrier specifications (material, cup count, fit range and carton quantity) are confirmed per order — contact us with your cup size and delivery setup for the right option.
