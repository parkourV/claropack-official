## 正文大纲与初稿

### 1. Why the Sealing Film Matters More Than You Think
For bubble tea and takeaway drinks, the sealing film is the single barrier between a full cup and a spilled delivery bag. A film that doesn't match your cup material or caliber leads to weak seals, peeling edges, and customer complaints. Getting the film right is a small decision with an outsized impact on delivery reliability and brand impression.

### 2. PP Film vs PET Film: Match the Film to the Cup
The core rule: **the sealing film should generally match the cup material** for a reliable heat seal.

- **PET film** pairs with **PET cold cups** (our 90–98mm calibers work with standard cup sealing machines using PET film).
- **PP film** pairs with **injection PP cups** (our 90/95mm round PP cups are designed for standard sealing machines with PP film).

### 3. Caliber Fit: The Film Must Match the Cup Rim
Just like lids, sealing film performance depends on the cup's rim diameter (caliber). Our sealing film is supplied for **90–98mm cups**, covering the most common boba and coffee cup sizes. Standardizing your shop around one or two calibers keeps a single film roll compatible across your whole menu.

> 待工厂确认：单卷膜的具体适配口径范围（是否一卷通配 90/95/98，或按口径分卷）。

### 4. Round vs Square Cups: A Sealing Difference to Plan For
Round cups (90/95mm) seal with standard round cup-sealing machines. Our **92mm square series** (650ml, 960ml) is a distinctive on-shelf format, but square cups require square-fit sealing rather than a standard round seal head — worth confirming with your machine before committing to square cups.

> 待工厂确认：方形杯是否提供配套的方形封口膜/封口方案，以及对封口机机型的要求。

### 5. Custom-Printed Film: A Low-Cost Branding Win
Sealing film is prime brand real estate — it's the first thing a customer sees when the drink arrives. We print your logo and artwork on **PP and PET sealing film rolls**. Because the film covers every cup regardless of size, custom film is one of the most cost-efficient ways to brand your whole menu at once.

> 待工厂确认：定制印刷膜的起订量（MOQ，按设计/按米/按卷）、可印刷色数、以及打样周期。

### 6. Machine Compatibility Checklist Before You Order
Before ordering film in bulk:
1. Confirm your cup material (PET or PP) and match the film material.
2. Confirm your cup caliber (90 / 95 / 98mm) — round cups use standard machines.
3. If using square cups (92mm), confirm square-fit sealing support.
4. Order a sample roll and test with your specific sealing machine before a bulk order.

## 3 条 FAQ（初稿）

1. **Q: Can I use PET film on PP cups (or vice versa)?**
   A: As a rule, match the film material to the cup material — PET film with PET cups, PP film with PP cups — for a reliable heat seal. If you need a single film across mixed materials, ask us to confirm compatibility before ordering.（"通用膜"是否存在 — 待工厂确认）

2. **Q: Which cup sizes does your sealing film fit?**
   A: Our sealing film is supplied for 90–98mm cups, which covers the most common boba and coffee cup calibers. Round 90/95mm cups run on standard cup-sealing machines.

3. **Q: Can the sealing film be custom printed with my logo?**
   A: Yes. We print custom logos and artwork on both PP and PET sealing film rolls. Since the film covers every cup size, it's a cost-efficient way to brand your entire menu.（起订量与打样周期 — 待工厂确认）
